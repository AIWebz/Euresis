Euresis Orbit Lab

A live model of known science: three equal masses moving under Newton's law of gravity, F = G·m₁m₂ / r². Euresis explored it for periodic orbits, rare motions that repeat exactly, and checked each against the largest published catalogue (Li & Liao, Shanghai Jiao Tong University).

Periodic orbits found

What the model is

Euresis' own law of universal gravitation, integrated numerically. Bodies start at (−1, 0), (1, 0) and (0, 0) with velocities (v₁, v₂), (v₁, v₂) and (−2v₁, −2v₂), the standard setting used in the literature, so results can be compared directly.

How Euresis explored it

Live physics and chaos

"Orbit" mode plays each orbit from a high-accuracy solution. "Live physics" integrates Newton's law in your browser from the same starting velocities. Raise "Nudge" to change the start very slightly and watch the bodies drift apart from the repeating path. That sensitivity is why periodic three-body orbits are so hard to find.
